Juntu device test page

index.html is a self-contained test page (no network requests, no external files). It checks which video encoding
route works on this device and how fast, by exporting short videos from generated test patterns (colour bars, test
signals and sine tones). One test can also use a video you pick yourself; it is read on the device only, and the
results record its format, not its name. Nothing is uploaded; results stay on the device until you copy or share them
yourself.
